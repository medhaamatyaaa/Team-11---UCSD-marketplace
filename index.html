About My Topic

Move-in and move-outs can be stressful, especially when you have to find a place to buy or sell the items you don't need anymore. TRITON SWAP is a platform that allows UCSD students to buy and sell items with each other, all in one place! We hope to make the process of buying and selling items easier for students, while also fostering a sense of community on campus. You upload your items, set your price, and wait for other students to contact you. It's that simple! We also have a donation feature where you can choose to donate your items to students that need it, if you simply want to get rid of them.
